London Travel Tips

Essential insider tips to make your London trip smoother, cheaper, and more memorable

💰 Money-Saving Tips

  • Get an Oyster card or use contactless — never buy single tickets. A daily cap means you'll never overpay for transport.
  • Free museums — The British Museum, Tate Modern, National Gallery, V&A, Natural History Museum, and Science Museum are all free. London has more free museums than any city on earth.
  • TKTS booth in Leicester Square — Get same-day West End theatre tickets at up to 50% off.
  • Eat at markets — Borough Market, Camden Market, and Maltby Street Market have incredible food at a fraction of restaurant prices.
  • Free walking tours — Operate on a tips basis. Excellent way to learn the city.
  • Lunchtime deals — Many restaurants offer set lunch menus at half the dinner price.
  • Tap water is free — All restaurants must provide free tap water by law.
  • Off-peak travel — Avoid the Tube between 7:30-9:30am and 5-7pm for cheaper fares and less crowding.

🔐 Safety & Practical Tips

  • London is very safe — but stay aware of pickpockets in tourist areas like Oxford Street, Camden Market, and the Tube.
  • Emergency number is 999 (or 112). For non-emergencies, call 101.
  • Pharmacies — Boots is everywhere and has pharmacists who can help with minor ailments without a doctor visit.
  • UK plugs are Type G — bring an adapter. Most hotels can lend one.
  • Tipping — 10-15% at restaurants is standard. Check if service charge is already included. No need to tip at pubs.
  • Contactless payment — accepted almost everywhere. Many places are card-only.

🇬🇧 British Etiquette

  • Queue properly — the British take queuing very seriously. Never skip the line.
  • Stand on the right on escalators — walk on the left. This is sacred Tube law.
  • Say "please" and "thank you" — politeness goes a very long way.
  • Don't talk loudly on the Tube — it's considered rude. Embrace the silence.
  • Let people off the train first — before boarding.
  • "Cheers" means "thanks" — and can also mean goodbye. You'll hear it constantly.

📱 Essential Apps

  • Citymapper — the best transport app for London. Better than Google Maps for public transport.
  • TfL Go — official Transport for London app with real-time Tube info.
  • TodayTix — last-minute theatre tickets at great prices.
  • OpenTable — restaurant reservations.
  • Too Good To Go — discounted meals from restaurants at closing time.
  • Uber / Bolt — for taxis. Black cabs are also excellent and use meters.
