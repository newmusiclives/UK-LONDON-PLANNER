🌸 Spring (March - May) — Best Overall

Temp: 8-17°C (46-63°F) | Crowds: Moderate | Prices: Mid-range

Parks bloom beautifully, days get longer, and the city comes alive. Cherry blossoms in Regent's Park and Kew Gardens are stunning. Easter brings special events. The Chelsea Flower Show in May is world-famous.

Best for: Gardens, walking, photography, moderate prices

☀️ Summer (June - August) — Peak Season

Temp: 15-25°C (59-77°F) | Crowds: High | Prices: Highest

Long days (light until 9:30pm), outdoor dining, rooftop bars, and festivals galore. Wimbledon in July, Notting Hill Carnival in August, Proms concerts at the Royal Albert Hall. Book everything well in advance.

Best for: Outdoor activities, festivals, late evenings. Book early — prices peak.

🍂 Autumn (September - November) — Hidden Gem

Temp: 7-18°C (45-64°F) | Crowds: Low-Moderate | Prices: Lower

Gorgeous autumn colours in the parks, fewer tourists, lower prices. September is still warm. The theatre season kicks off with new shows. Bonfire Night (5 Nov) features spectacular fireworks along the Thames.

Best for: Budget travel, museums, theatre, autumn walks, fewer crowds

❄️ Winter (December - February) — Festive Magic

Temp: 2-8°C (36-46°F) | Crowds: Low (except Christmas) | Prices: Lowest (except Christmas)

Christmas lights on Oxford Street and Regent Street are magical. Winter Wonderland in Hyde Park, ice skating at Somerset House, and pantomime season. January sales offer incredible shopping deals. February is the cheapest month to visit.

Best for: Christmas markets, shopping sales, cosy pubs, lowest prices (Jan-Feb)
