Bankside Neighbourhood Guide

Tate Modern, Shakespeare's Globe, and Borough Market — the Thames's most culturally packed half-mile

Overview

Bankside is the stretch of the south bank of the Thames between Blackfriars Bridge and London Bridge — once a notorious theatre and red-light district during Shakespeare's time, now the city's most culturally dense riverside.

The core: Tate Modern (in the old Bankside Power Station), Shakespeare's Globe (a 1997 reconstruction), Borough Market (1,000 years old), the Millennium Bridge to St Paul's, and a direct Thames-Path walk to Tower Bridge.

The vibe: Buzzing and tourist-happy by day; surprisingly quiet Sunday evenings; a Londoners-favourite walk on summer Fridays.

Best for: First-timers, culture seekers, foodies, photographers, families with kids.

Top Attractions

  • 1. Tate Modern

    Bankside, SE1 9TG

    The UK's national gallery of modern and contemporary art, in a former power station. Free entry. The Turbine Hall's rotating installations are iconic.

  • 2. Shakespeare's Globe

    21 New Globe Walk, SE1 9DT

    1997 reconstruction of the 1599 theatre — authentic open-air staging, £5 "groundling" standing tickets, season April-October. Guided tours in winter.

  • 3. Borough Market

    8 Southwark St, SE1 1TL

    One of London's oldest food markets (13th century). Weekends busiest; weekdays calmer. 100+ stalls and a legendary food scene around the edges.

  • 4. Millennium Bridge

    Thames Embankment, SE1 9JE

    The elegant steel-cable footbridge linking St Paul's to Tate Modern — London's first new Thames crossing in over a century. Famously wobbled on opening.

  • 5. The Clink Prison Museum

    1 Clink St, SE1 9DG

    Remnants of England's oldest prison (1144-1780) — on the site, atmospheric, good for teenagers.

Best Restaurants

  • 1. Padella

    6 Southwark St, SE1 1TQ · Italian · $$

    Hand-made pasta at incredibly reasonable prices. The pappardelle with 8-hour beef shin ragu is extraordinary. No reservations — queue.

  • 2. Hawksmoor Borough

    16 Winchester Walk, SE1 9AQ · British · $$$$

    Legendary British steakhouse in a vast glass-roofed Victorian room. Express menu £30.

  • 3. Rambutan

    10 Stoney St, SE1 9AD · Sri Lankan · $$$

    Cynthia Shanmugalingam's modern Sri Lankan — hot-pink booth seats, banana-leaf plates, ambitious flavours.

  • 4. Arabica Bar & Kitchen

    Rochester Walk, SE1 9AF · Middle Eastern · $$$

    Borough Market meze restaurant — mezze, grills, all day. Great for groups.

  • 5. Roast

    The Floral Hall, SE1 1TL · British · $$$

    Directly above Borough Market — glass-walled dining room, British roast trolley, tourist-friendly Sunday lunch.

Best Bars & Pubs

  • 1. The Anchor Bankside

    34 Park St, SE1 9EF

    Grade II-listed Thames-side pub — Shakespeare drank here, Pepys escaped the Great Fire here. Riverside terrace is the summer draw.

  • 2. The George Inn

    77 Borough High St, SE1 1NH

    London's last surviving galleried coaching inn (1677) — Dickens drank here, the National Trust preserves it. Atmospheric cobbled courtyard.

  • 3. Menier Chocolate Factory Bar

    53-55 Southwark St, SE1 1RU

    Former cocoa warehouse's theatre bar — excellent cocktails, dinner-and-show packages.

  • 4. Aqua Shard

    31 St Thomas St, SE1 9RY

    Level 31 of the Shard — book just the bar for panoramic skyline views without the restaurant spend.

  • 5. The Rake

    14a Winchester Walk, SE1 9AG

    Borough Market craft beer pioneer — 12+ taps, tiny bench-seated snug, expert staff.

Best Cafes

  • 1. Monmouth Coffee (Borough)

    2 Park St, SE1 9AB

    London's original specialty coffee roaster. Legendary. Queues at weekends but move fast.

  • 2. Bread Ahead Borough

    86 Borough High St, SE1 1LL

    Market bakery famous for doughnuts — vanilla-custard classics, bread classes upstairs.

  • 3. Fernandez & Wells Somerset House

    Strand, WC2R 1LA

    Technically north of the river but the nearest quality café to Bankside. Pulled-pork sandwich is a cult pick.

Shopping Highlights

  • Borough Market — food market, 100+ stalls. Saturdays are the biggest day; Thursdays calmer.
  • Tate Modern Shop — excellent contemporary art books, prints, design objects.
  • Vinegar Yard — post-industrial street-food and events space with an antiques flea market on Saturdays and Sundays.
  • Maltby Street Market (10-min walk east) — the food-lovers' alternative to Borough, Saturdays only.
  • Utobeer — Borough Market specialist craft-beer stall with 400+ beers to take home.

Getting There

Main stations serving this neighbourhood:

  • London Bridge (Northern, Jubilee) — east side — Borough Market-adjacent; also mainline
  • Southwark (Jubilee) — centre — Tate Modern and Globe closest
  • Blackfriars (District, Circle) — north-west — also a mainline station on the Thames itself
  • Waterloo (Bakerloo, Northern, Jubilee, Waterloo & City) — west side — 15-min walk along the Thames Path

Insider Tips

  • Tate Modern's Level 10 viewing gallery is free — sunset over St Paul's is spectacular.
  • Globe standing (groundling) tickets are £5 — arrive early, but the 2.5-hour stand is authentic.
  • Borough Market is Thursday-Saturday (10am-5pm); early Saturday is busiest. Tuesday-Wednesday stalls are limited.
  • Walk the Thames Path from Bankside to Tower Bridge at sunset — 20 minutes, spectacular views.
  • Vinegar Yard's rotating street-food traders open weekend evenings — a less touristy alternative to Borough.
