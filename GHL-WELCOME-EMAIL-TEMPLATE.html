TIP #1

The £1.75 Sightseeing Tour

Skip the £35 tour buses. Bus 11 from Liverpool Street passes St Paul's, the Strand, Trafalgar Square, Westminster, and the Houses of Parliament — for just £1.75.

The Hopper fare means unlimited bus transfers within 1 hour on a single fare. Jump off at St Paul's for photos, catch the next bus — still £1.75.

Saves: £30+ per person

TIP #2

The West End Theatre Hack

TKTS booth in Leicester Square: same-day tickets at 20-50% off. It's the official discount booth run by the Society of London Theatre — not a scam.

Day seats: Hamilton, Les Mis, and Phantom release £20-25 tickets at the box office each morning. Queue from 8am.

Best nights: Tuesday and Wednesday are cheapest. Friday/Saturday most expensive. Same show, same cast.

Saves: £40-80 per ticket

TIP #3

London's Free Museum Secret

London has more free museums than any city on Earth. The British Museum, Tate Modern, National Gallery, V&A, Natural History Museum, and Science Museum are all completely free.

Timing trick: Go weekday mornings (10am-12pm) or Friday evenings (many open late until 8:30pm) for fewer crowds.

Saves: £100+ over a week

BONUS: The Lunch Hack

London's best restaurants offer set lunch menus at 40-60% off dinner prices. Same chef, same kitchen. Eat your big meal at lunch, have a pub dinner. You'll eat better for less.
