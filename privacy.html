Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly: name, email address, phone number (optional), trip preferences, and payment details. Payment processing is handled securely by Manifest Financial — we never store your full card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: generate personalised itineraries, process payments, send booking confirmations and trip-related communications, provide customer support, and improve our services. With your consent, we may send promotional emails with London travel tips.

3. CRM and Communications

We use GoHighLevel as our customer relationship management platform to manage email communications, SMS notifications (with your consent), and customer support. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Manifest Financial — payment processing
  • GoHighLevel — CRM, email, SMS communications
  • Affiliate partners (GetYourGuide, Booking.com, Viator, etc.) — when you click booking links, their privacy policies apply
  • OpenStreetMap — map data
  • OpenWeatherMap — weather data
  • TfL — transport status data

5. Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to save your itinerary preferences and generated itineraries locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser unless you explicitly share it. We use essential cookies only — no advertising cookies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS. Payment data is processed directly by Manifest Financial and never passes through our servers.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your personal data, request correction or deletion, opt out of marketing communications, and request a copy of your data. Contact us at hello@londonjourneyplanner.com for any privacy-related requests.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, contact: hello@londonjourneyplanner.com
