Best Day Trips from London by Train

12 brilliant escapes — all under 2 hours from central London, no car needed

🚂 Tips Before You Go

  • Book in advance for the cheapest fares — Trainline and National Rail often have 50%+ off advance tickets.
  • Travel off-peak (after 9:30am) for cheaper tickets and emptier trains.
  • Get a Railcard if you're doing multiple trips — 1/3 off all fares. The 16-25, 26-30, Two Together, and Family railcards are all great value.
  • Use contactless at London terminals — you don't need a separate ticket for the London end.

1. Brighton

1hrFrom £12

From London Victoria or London Bridge · Best for: couples, foodies, beach lovers

London's favourite seaside escape. The bohemian Lanes are packed with independent shops and restaurants, the Royal Pavilion is magnificently bizarre, and the seafront has a classic pier with fish and chips. Don't miss the North Laine for vintage shopping and Terre a Terre for vegetarian fine dining.

2. Oxford

1hrFrom £10

From London Paddington · Best for: history buffs, Harry Potter fans, culture seekers

The "City of Dreaming Spires" delivers world-class colleges, the Bodleian Library, covered market, and Ashmolean Museum (free). Christ Church College was used for Harry Potter filming. Punt on the Cherwell in summer. The Eagle and Child pub was Tolkien and C.S. Lewis's local.

3. Cambridge

50minFrom £10

From London King's Cross · Best for: couples, architecture lovers, families

Punting on the River Cam is the quintessential Cambridge experience. King's College Chapel is breathtaking, the Fitzwilliam Museum is free and exceptional, and the Backs (gardens behind the colleges) are stunning. Hire a punt from Scudamore's — or let someone else do the work with a chauffeured punt.

4. Bath

1hr 30minFrom £15

From London Paddington · Best for: couples, history buffs, spa lovers

A UNESCO World Heritage city of honey-coloured Georgian architecture. The Roman Baths are fascinating, the Royal Crescent is architecturally perfect, and Thermae Bath Spa has a rooftop pool with city views. Sally Lunn's serves the famous Bath bun in the oldest house in the city.

5. Canterbury

1hrFrom £12

From London St Pancras (high speed) · Best for: history buffs, families

Canterbury Cathedral is magnificent — the site of Thomas Becket's murder in 1170. The medieval city centre is compact and walkable. The Canterbury Tales attraction brings Chaucer to life. The Goods Shed is a brilliant farmers' market in a converted train shed right next to the station.

6. Margate

1hr 30minFrom £15

From London St Pancras (high speed) · Best for: art lovers, beach seekers, creative types

Margate has reinvented itself as a creative seaside town. Turner Contemporary gallery (free) overlooks the beach that inspired the painter. The Old Town has vintage shops, galleries, and excellent restaurants. Dreamland is a retro amusement park. The Shell Grotto is genuinely mysterious.

7. Whitstable

1hr 20minFrom £14

From London Victoria · Best for: foodies, couples, relaxation

Famous for its oysters and charming harbour. The Oyster Company for fresh oysters on the beach, Wheelers Oyster Bar for the sit-down experience. Walk along the shingle beach, browse the colourful fisherman's huts, and visit the independent shops on Harbour Street.

8. Winchester

1hrFrom £12

From London Waterloo · Best for: history buffs, families

England's ancient capital. Winchester Cathedral is stunning (Jane Austen is buried here), the Great Hall houses the legendary Round Table, and the water meadows walk is one of England's finest. The Chesil Rectory is a medieval building serving excellent modern British food.

9. Rye

1hrFrom £15

From London St Pancras (change at Ashford) · Best for: couples, photographers

A perfectly preserved medieval town with cobbled streets, half-timbered houses, and a harbour. Mermaid Street is one of England's most photographed streets. The Mermaid Inn has been serving since 1420. Climb St Mary's Church tower for panoramic views across Romney Marsh to the sea.

10. Stratford-upon-Avon

2hrFrom £14

From London Marylebone · Best for: theatre lovers, history buffs, families

Shakespeare's birthplace. Visit his childhood home, see a show at the Royal Shakespeare Company (book ahead), and walk along the River Avon. Anne Hathaway's cottage is a 30-minute walk through fields. The town is compact and charming with Tudor buildings lining the streets.

11. Bristol

1hr 45minFrom £15

From London Paddington · Best for: street art fans, foodies, creative types

Banksy's hometown is bursting with creativity. The harbourside area has great restaurants and the SS Great Britain. Stokes Croft is the street art capital. Clifton Suspension Bridge is Brunel's masterpiece. St Nicholas Market is packed with independent food stalls. Bristol's food scene rivals London's.

12. Hastings

1hr 30minFrom £14

From London St Pancras (high speed) or Charing Cross · Best for: families, history buffs, budget travellers

More than just 1066. The Old Town is full of character with unique "net shops" (tall black wooden huts), a funicular railway, the Jerwood Gallery of modern art, and Europe's largest beach-launched fishing fleet. The fish and chips from Maggie's are legendary. Take the East Hill Lift for cliff-top views.
