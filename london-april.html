London in April

Cherry blossoms, the London Marathon, Easter celebrations, and glorious spring weather make April one of the best months to visit London.

🌡️ Weather Overview

April is when London truly shakes off winter. The weather is mild, though famously unpredictable:

  • Average temperature: 6°C to 14°C (43°F to 57°F), with warm days occasionally reaching 18-20°C
  • Rainfall: Around 45mm across 12 rainy days — "April showers" are real but rarely last long
  • Daylight hours: 13 to 15 hours — long, bright days with sunrise before 6am and sunset after 8pm by late April
  • Sunshine: Around 5.5 hours of sun per day, with increasing warmth

The classic "four seasons in one day" description fits April perfectly. You might start the morning in sunshine, get a sharp shower at lunchtime, and be sitting in a pub garden by 3pm. The key is layers and flexibility.

🎭 What's On in April

April is packed with events as London shakes off hibernation:

  • London Marathon (late Apr): One of the world's great marathons, with 40,000+ runners from elite athletes to fancy-dress fundraisers. Watch for free from Greenwich, Tower Bridge, or The Mall. The atmosphere is incredible.
  • Easter Weekend (date varies): Easter egg hunts in Kensington Palace Gardens, special services at St Paul's Cathedral and Westminster Abbey, and the London Easter Parade of floats and performers.
  • Shakespeare's Globe Season Opens (late Apr): The open-air theatre season begins on the South Bank. Groundling tickets (standing) are just five pounds — one of London's best cultural bargains.
  • Cherry Blossom Season (throughout Apr): London's parks and gardens explode in pink and white. Top spots include Greenwich Park, Kew Gardens, Regent's Park, and the avenue along St James's Park.
  • London Games Festival (early Apr): A citywide celebration of video games culture with events at BAFTA, the Barbican, and venues across London.
  • Underbelly Festival Opens (late Apr): The Southbank Centre's outdoor festival of comedy, circus, cabaret, and street food kicks off, running through September. The riverside Spiegeltent is a highlight.
  • Vaisakhi Festival (mid-Apr): The Sikh New Year is celebrated with a vibrant parade and festival in Trafalgar Square, featuring music, dance, martial arts displays, and free vegetarian food.
  • Rooftop Bars Open for the Season: Many of London's best rooftop venues — including the Skylight bars in Tobacco Dock and Peckham, Madison near St Paul's, and the Boundary Rooftop in Shoreditch — open for the warm months.

✨ Top Things to Do in April

April opens up the outdoors while still offering great value before peak summer season:

  • Chase the cherry blossoms: Greenwich Park's cherry tree avenue is the most Instagrammed, but the Regent's Park avenue near Chester Road is equally beautiful and less crowded. Kew Gardens' Cherry Walk is magnificent.
  • Watch the London Marathon: No ticket needed — just find a spot on the route. Tower Bridge and Canary Wharf offer dramatic backdrops. The Mall near Buckingham Palace is where the finish-line drama unfolds.
  • Explore Hampstead Heath in spring: The swimming ponds open in April (for the brave). The wildflower meadows begin blooming, and the views from Parliament Hill on a clear April morning are breathtaking.
  • Take a walking tour: April's mild weather makes walking tours ideal. Try a Jack the Ripper tour through Whitechapel, a street art tour of Shoreditch, or a Royal London walk through Westminster.
  • Visit Hampton Court Palace: The gardens are spectacular in April, with the Great Vine in leaf and the privy garden in bloom. The palace itself is one of England's finest Tudor buildings.
  • Stand as a groundling at the Globe: Pay five pounds to stand in the yard of Shakespeare's open-air theatre, just as Elizabethan audiences did. It's one of London's most unique cultural experiences.
  • Picnic in Regent's Park: The rose garden won't peak until June, but the park is gorgeous in April. Queen Mary's Gardens, the boating lake, and the Broad Walk are all worth exploring.
  • Visit the Sky Garden for free: Book free tickets online for the 35th-floor Sky Garden at 20 Fenchurch Street ("The Walkie Talkie"). Panoramic views and a lush garden — book at least 2 weeks ahead.
  • Explore Brick Lane on a Sunday: The vintage market, curry houses, bagel shops (Beigel Bake is the original), and street art make this the quintessential East London experience.
  • Cycle through Hyde Park and Kensington Gardens: Santander Cycles ("Boris Bikes") are perfect for a spring ride through the connected Royal Parks. Stop at the Serpentine Gallery and the Diana Memorial Fountain.

🍽️ Where to Eat & Drink

April menus celebrate spring with lighter, brighter flavours:

  • The Ledbury, Notting Hill: Brett Graham's two-Michelin-star restaurant showcases British spring ingredients — expect dishes featuring wild garlic, asparagus, spring lamb, and morels. A special occasion destination.
  • E Pellicci, Bethnal Green: A Grade II-listed Italian cafe serving legendary fry-ups and pasta since 1900. The Formica interior, warm welcome, and honest cooking are pure London. Cash only.
  • Frank's Cafe, Peckham: A rooftop bar atop a multi-storey car park with extraordinary views across the London skyline. Opens in spring with natural wine, craft beer, and a laid-back South London vibe.
  • The Eagle, Farringdon: Britain's original gastropub (opened 1991) still serves bold, Mediterranean-influenced food from an open kitchen. The steak sandwich is legendary. No reservations.
  • Petersham Nurseries Cafe, Richmond: Dine among the greenhouses and plant displays at this romantic, rustically elegant restaurant. The seasonal Italian menu uses produce from their own gardens. Book well ahead.
  • The Windmill, Mayfair: A proper pie pub in the heart of Mayfair. Their award-winning pies — from steak and kidney to venison — are handmade and excellent. The roof terrace opens in April.

💡 Insider Tips

  • Easter weekend pushes up prices: Hotel rates and flight prices spike over the Easter bank holiday weekend. If your dates are flexible, travel the week before or after Easter for significantly better value.
  • Marathon Sunday affects transport: On London Marathon day (usually the last Sunday in April), large parts of southeast and central London are closed to traffic. Plan around it or embrace it as a spectator.
  • Book Globe tickets early: Groundling tickets are cheap but popular. Book online as soon as the season is announced. Matinee performances are best — you'll catch the daylight.
  • Cherry blossoms peak for 1-2 weeks: The exact timing depends on the year's weather. Follow @kikiandtea or @cherryblossomlondon on Instagram for real-time bloom updates.
  • Late April is ideal: If you can choose your dates, late April offers the longest days, the warmest weather, and peak cherry blossom season — all before May's higher prices kick in.

🎒 What to Pack

  • Light waterproof jacket — essential for April showers
  • Layers: t-shirts, a jumper, and a light jacket
  • Comfortable walking shoes for parks and cobblestones
  • Sunglasses and SPF — the spring sun can catch you off guard
  • Small daypack for carrying layers as temperatures fluctuate
  • Compact umbrella
  • Picnic blanket if you plan to enjoy the parks
