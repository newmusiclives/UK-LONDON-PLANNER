🚇 The Tube (Underground)

London's iconic underground railway is the fastest way to get around the city. 11 lines cover 270 stations across London.

  • Hours: 5am-midnight (some lines run 24hr on Fridays & Saturdays — the "Night Tube")
  • Cost: £2.80 per journey with Oyster/contactless (Zone 1). Daily cap: £8.10
  • Top tip: Avoid rush hour (7:30-9:30am, 5-7pm) for a much better experience
  • Night Tube: Victoria, Central, Jubilee, Northern, and Piccadilly lines run all night on Fri/Sat

🚌 Buses

London's iconic red buses cover routes the Tube doesn't reach, and you get to see the city as you travel.

  • Cost: £1.75 per journey (Oyster/contactless only — no cash accepted)
  • Hopper fare: Unlimited bus transfers within 1 hour for one fare
  • Routes 11 and 15: Pass major landmarks — a budget sightseeing tour
  • Night buses: Run all night (prefix "N" — e.g., N11)

💳 Oyster Cards & Contactless

The most affordable way to use London transport.

  • Contactless: Just tap your bank card — same price as Oyster. Works with Apple Pay and Google Pay.
  • Oyster card: £7 deposit from any Tube station. Good if you don't have contactless.
  • Daily cap: You'll never pay more than £8.10/day (Zones 1-2) no matter how many journeys.
  • Weekly cap: £40.70 for Zones 1-2 with contactless (Mon-Sun).
  • Children under 11: Travel free with an adult on Tube, buses, and trains.

🚶 Walking

London is incredibly walkable. Many "nearby" Tube stations are just 5-10 minutes apart on foot.

  • South Bank walk: Westminster Bridge to Tower Bridge — 3km of stunning riverside views
  • Covent Garden to Soho: 10-minute walk through the heart of the West End
  • Regent's Canal: Camden to King's Cross — a beautiful towpath walk
  • Thames Path: 184-mile trail following the river — walk any section

🚕 Taxis & Rideshares

  • Black cabs: Iconic and excellent. Drivers know every street. Hail one with its light on, or use the Gett app.
  • Uber / Bolt: Usually 20-40% cheaper than black cabs. Available everywhere.
  • Late night: After midnight, taxis/rideshares are often the best option.

🚲 Cycling

  • Santander Cycles ("Boris Bikes"): £1.65 for 30-min rides. Docking stations everywhere.
  • Lime / Forest e-bikes: Available via app. Great for longer journeys.
  • Best routes: Regent's Park, Hyde Park, along the Thames, and the Canals.

⛴️ River Boats

  • Uber Boat by Thames Clippers: Fast catamaran service along the Thames. Oyster/contactless accepted.
  • Great for: Westminster to Greenwich, or Embankment to Canary Wharf.
  • Cost: £8-9 single, or included in some travel passes.
