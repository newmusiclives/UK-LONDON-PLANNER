Two things worth sorting early: how you get from the airport and travel insurance. Both can wait until the last minute — but both cost 2–3× more when you do.

Airport transfers — Heathrow / Gatwick

Best options, in order:

  • Heathrow Express (15 min to Paddington) — ~£25 advance, ~£45 on the day
  • Elizabeth Line (35 min to central, £12.80) — brilliant value, runs till midnight
  • Pre-booked transfer car — £55–£75, door-to-door, great for groups or late arrivals

Travel insurance

Medical care in the UK is not free for visitors. A twisted ankle at the Tower of London = £400+ without cover. The two we use ourselves:

  • World Nomads — flexible, adventure sports included, good for 2–3 week trips
  • SafetyWing — best for long trips or if you're extending to Europe
