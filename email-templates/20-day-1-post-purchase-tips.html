Quick post-purchase pack — three things to sort out this week, while it's fresh in your head.

1. ESTA + UK ETA

US passport holders need both. ESTA is $21 (esta.cbp.dhs.gov), UK ETA is £10 (gov.uk/apply-uk-eta). Approval is usually same-day, but allow 72 hours. Do it now so it's not a worry.

2. A no-FX card

If you don't already have a Chase Sapphire, Capital One Venture, Wise, or Revolut — now's a great time. Foreign transaction fees on a regular debit card will cost you £50+ on a London trip. Wise is fastest to set up (app, 10 minutes).

3. Packing list (short version)

A compact waterproof jacket. Proper walking shoes. An umbrella you don't mind losing. Layers. A power adapter (UK is type G, 3-pin). US-to-UK adapters are £15 at Heathrow but £5 on Amazon beforehand.
