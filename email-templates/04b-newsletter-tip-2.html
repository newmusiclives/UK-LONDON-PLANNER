Where Americans should actually stay

Most first-time visitors book Leicester Square or Piccadilly because they're on the map. Locals avoid those areas — they're expensive, touristy, and empty at 9pm.

Our three picks:

Fitzrovia — Central but calm. Walk to Soho, Oxford St, British Museum. Great indie restaurants. Best for first-timers.

Bloomsbury — Bookish, leafy, 10 minutes to the West End. Walk to the British Museum, easy Tube to anywhere. Best for couples and culture travellers.

Shoreditch — Food, nightlife, markets, street art. 15 min to the City, 20 to the West End. Best for returning visitors who want a different London.

What to avoid: Zone 3+ hotels that look cheap online — the daily Tube time adds up fast. Also skip Earls Court and Bayswater (dated, quiet, far).
