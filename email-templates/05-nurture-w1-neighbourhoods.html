The neighbourhoods Americans always get wrong (and where to actually stay)

Every week on the waitlist, we'll send one genuinely useful thing about London that travel sites don't tell you. This week: where to stay — and more importantly, where not to.

The trap: Leicester Square, Piccadilly, Bayswater, Earls Court.

They show up on "best for tourists" lists because they're central on the map. In reality they're overpriced, dead after 9pm, and surrounded by chain restaurants. You'll feel like you're in a theme park.

Where to stay instead:

  • Fitzrovia or Bloomsbury — walkable to the West End, British Museum, Oxford Street. Leafy, calm, proper local restaurants.
  • Marylebone — villagey, independent boutiques, some of the city's best food, walk to Regent's Park.
  • Shoreditch — if you want markets, street food, nightlife. 15 min to the City, 20 to West End.
  • Bermondsey / Borough — Thames-side, foodie heaven, walk to the South Bank attractions.

One rule: stay in Zones 1–2. If the hotel is "great value" but in Zone 3+, you'll burn 45 minutes each way on the Tube and £20/day in fares.
