Two weeks. Packing time.

London weather is mild and changeable — think Seattle, not Phoenix. The single biggest mistake Americans make is packing too warm or too little rainproof kit.

The essentials

  • Compact waterproof jacket — the single most-used item on your trip
  • Proper walking shoes — you'll clock 15,000–25,000 steps a day
  • Layers — a tee + light jumper + jacket is more versatile than one warm coat
  • A small backpack or crossbody — hands free for the Tube, rain, photos
  • UK Type-G plug adapter (3 rectangular pins) — £5 on Amazon now, £15 at Heathrow
  • Portable charger — 10,000 mAh minimum, you'll need it by 4pm every day

What not to pack

  • Heavy winter coats (unless December–February)
  • Big umbrellas — useless in London wind. Compact ones only.
  • Formal clothes — most London restaurants are smart-casual
  • Too many shoes — one walking pair, one smart pair, done

The sneaky useful items

  • A small flask — pub gardens don't mind if you BYO occasionally
  • A reusable water bottle — London tap water is excellent and free refills everywhere
  • Tide pen / stain wipes — one chip shop lunch in and you'll be glad
