It's been a month since you got back from London. If the post-trip itch has kicked in (it always does) — here's where London-lovers usually go next.

Edinburgh — if you loved London's history

Scotland's capital: a medieval old town on top of an extinct volcano, with better whisky and the best festival (August). 4.5h by train. Do it in 3–5 days.

Dublin — if you loved the pubs

1h flight, 2.5h ferry. Literary history, live music in every pub, Guinness Storehouse, day-trips to the Cliffs of Moher. 3 days is enough for the city, 5 for the country.

Paris — if you loved the food

2h15 on the Eurostar from St Pancras. A completely different energy — denser, more ornate, less green. 4 days minimum, 7 ideal.

Amsterdam — if you loved the neighbourhoods

3h40 on the Eurostar direct. Canals, bicycles, museums, cafés — a walkable city like London was 100 years ago. 3–4 days.
