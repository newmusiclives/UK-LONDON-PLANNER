Tube, Oyster & Black Cabs: your no-stress guide to getting around London

Getting around London is genuinely easy once you know the rules. Here's the whole system in 4 minutes.

1. Tap contactless, skip Oyster

Any contactless card or Apple/Google Pay taps on the yellow reader. Fares cap at the daily Travelcard rate (£8.50 for Zones 1–2). You don't need a Visitor Oyster unless you're under 18. Use a no-foreign-fee card — Wise, Revolut, Chase Sapphire are fine.

2. Tube for distance, walk for 1–2 stops

Most central Tube stations are 5–8 minutes apart on foot. If Citymapper says "20 min walk", it's often faster than Tubing. Two exceptions: Tottenham Court Rd ↔ Holborn and Bank ↔ Monument are 2–3 min walks underground.

3. Buses beat Tube at street level

Double-deckers, top deck, front row — free sightseeing tour. Route 11 (Liverpool St to Fulham) and Route 15 (Trafalgar Sq to Tower Hill) pass more landmarks than most paid tours. Same contactless tap. £1.75 flat, or free if you tap within an hour of your last bus.

4. Black cabs vs Uber

Black cabs know the city without GPS and are worth the extra £3–5 for late nights or complex routes. Uber is fine in daytime. Ignore minicabs with no ID — illegal and unsafe.

5. Avoid 8–9am and 5–7pm

Rush hour is genuinely awful. Plan museums and indoor activities for those windows, outdoor walks for 10am–4pm.
