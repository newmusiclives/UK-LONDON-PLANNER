UK rail — book now, save a lot

You're extending into the UK — {{contact.uk_destinations}}. UK rail is split into two pricing worlds:

  • Advance tickets — released ~12 weeks out, £15–£50 typical. Fixed train, no flexibility.
  • Anytime tickets — book on the day, £80–£200 typical. Any train, full flexibility.

If your dates are locked, go Advance. The same London→Edinburgh ticket is £38 booked early, £165 on the day.
