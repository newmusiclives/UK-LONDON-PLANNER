10 things Americans wish they'd known before their first London trip

  1. Stand on the right on escalators. Left is for walking. Block the left and you'll get a very British sigh.
  2. Pubs close at 11pm. Many at 10pm on Sundays. Last orders happen 20 minutes earlier. Dinner = earlier than in NYC.
  3. You don't tip like at home. Restaurants: 12.5% service is usually already on the bill. Pubs: no tip at all. Taxis: round up.
  4. Sunday trading laws are real. Big shops open 11am–5pm only. Small shops and markets are fine.
  5. Museums are free. British Museum, Tate, National Gallery, V&A, Natural History — all free entry. Pay £5 for the audio guide if you want.
  6. "Ground floor" = first floor. The first floor is what you'd call the second floor. Elevator says "G" for ground.
  7. Weather changes every hour. Bring a light waterproof jacket every day, even in July. It's rarely very hot, rarely very cold.
  8. Portions are smaller. Not stingy — that's normal. Order a side if you're hungry. No free refills on sodas.
  9. Don't call a cab, hail one. Orange "Taxi" light = available. Step to the kerb and raise a hand.
  10. ESTA, not visa. US passport holders need an ESTA online ($21) plus a new UK ETA. Do both at least 72 hours before you fly.
