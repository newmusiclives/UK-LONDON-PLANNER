London by season: what's on, what to pack, what to skip

London is a different city in every season. Here's how to play each one.

Spring (Mar–May)

What's on: Chelsea Flower Show (late May), cherry blossom at Greenwich, Regent's Park gardens at peak.

Pack: Layers, a compact umbrella, waterproof shoes.

Skip: Easter Monday if you hate crowds — everything open plus extra tourists.

Summer (Jun–Aug)

What's on: Wimbledon (late Jun), Proms (Jul–Sep), Notting Hill Carnival (Aug bank holiday), outdoor theatre in Regent's Park.

Pack: Light layers. A warm evening jacket. The Tube has no air con.

Skip: Inside-the-Tube commutes at rush hour. 35°C on the Central line is a core memory you don't need.

Autumn (Sep–Nov)

What's on: Frieze Art Fair (Oct), Bonfire Night (Nov 5 — fireworks everywhere), London Film Festival.

Pack: Warm coat, scarf, decent boots.

Skip: Nothing. Autumn in London is magical and underrated.

Winter (Dec–Feb)

What's on: Winter Wonderland Hyde Park, Christmas lights on Oxford & Regent St, ice skating at Somerset House & Tower of London, New Year's fireworks on the Thames.

Pack: Proper coat, gloves, warm socks. It rarely snows but damp + wind = cold.

Skip: Outdoor-only itineraries. Mix indoor museums + cosy pubs.
