Thirty days from today you're in London. Here's the sharp-end checklist.

This week

  • Check your passport. UK requires at least 3 months validity past return date — 6 is safer. Renew now if it's tight.
  • Apply for ESTA — esta.cbp.dhs.gov, $21. Same-day approval usually.
  • Apply for UK ETA — gov.uk/apply-uk-eta, £10. Needed from April 2025.

Next two weeks

  • Order a Wise or Revolut card — delivery takes ~7 days. Or use Chase Sapphire if you have one.
  • Book the big restaurants in your itinerary — the icons (Dishoom, St. JOHN, Sketch) fill 4–6 weeks out.
  • Buy travel insurance if you haven't — World Nomads or SafetyWing.

Anytime before you fly

  • Download Citymapper and Google Maps offline for central London.
  • Get a UK eSIM (Airalo) if you don't have international roaming.
  • Screenshot your itinerary — belts-and-braces if Wi-Fi fails.
