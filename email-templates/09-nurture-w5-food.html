The London food scene: beyond fish and chips

London eats better than almost any city in the world now. Here's how to find the good stuff, whatever your budget.

Cheap-and-brilliant (£10–£20)

  • Padella (Borough) — fresh pasta, under £13, queue but worth it
  • Honest Burgers — the rosemary-salted chips are an institution
  • Maltby Street Market (Saturdays) — gin, pies, salt-beef bagels, all under £12
  • Dishoom — bacon naan at breakfast, £7, life-changing

Mid-range gems (£25–£50)

  • Brat — wood-fired, whole turbot, unfussy genius
  • Gloria (Shoreditch) — Italian chaos, fantastic carbonara cone
  • Berenjak (Soho) — Persian grills, extraordinary rice
  • Kiln (Soho) — Thai counter cooking, short menu, long queue, worth it

Bucket-list splurges (£80+)

  • St. JOHN — nose-to-tail British, changed global cooking
  • The Araki — 9-seat sushi counter, book months ahead
  • Sketch — the pink Gallery is Instagram's London
  • Rules — London's oldest restaurant (1798), proper British game

The trick: match the meal to the mood. Big day of walking = fresh pasta. Rainy afternoon = gastropub. Anniversary = St. JOHN. Your London Planned itinerary does this automatically, based on your schedule and budget.
