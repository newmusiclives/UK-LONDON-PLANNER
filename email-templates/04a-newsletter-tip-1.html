The Tube trick that saves you £100+

Skip the Travelcard. Skip paper tickets. Just tap your contactless bank card or phone on the yellow reader — it fares-cap automatically at the daily Travelcard rate.

Why this matters: a 7-day Travelcard is £42.70. A Visitor Oyster costs £5 just for the card. Contactless caps hit the same £42.70 but with no upfront fee, no top-ups, and no faff on arrival.

One catch: make sure it's a foreign-exchange-fee-free card (Wise, Revolut, Chase Sapphire, most travel cards). Otherwise a 3% fee eats the savings.
