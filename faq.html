Frequently Asked Questions

How does the itinerary builder work? ▾

You answer a few questions about your trip — how many days, your interests, budget, and who's travelling. Our engine then creates a personalised day-by-day itinerary from our database of 800+ London venues, tailored to match your preferences.

What do I get when I purchase? ▾

You get a complete day-by-day itinerary with morning, afternoon, and evening activities, restaurant and pub recommendations, insider tips, an interactive map, a downloadable PDF guide, a smart packing list, and booking links for all venues.

Can I preview before paying? ▾

Yes! The first 2 days of your itinerary are always free to preview. You can see the quality and detail before deciding to purchase the full guide.

How is payment processed? ▾

Payments are securely processed through Manifest Financial. We accept all major credit and debit cards. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.

What is the Booking Concierge? ▾

For $250, our team will make as many bookings and reservations as possible on your behalf — restaurants, attractions, theatre tickets, tours. If something is unavailable, we'll find the best alternative. You receive a confirmation email with all booking details.

What is the Personal Consultation? ▾

A 1-hour private video call with a London expert who'll customise your itinerary, share insider tips, make restaurant bookings, and answer all your questions. You also get a follow-up email with notes and 7 days of priority email support.

Can I get a refund? ▾

Due to the digital nature of our itineraries, we don't offer refunds on itinerary purchases. Consultation bookings can be refunded if cancelled 48+ hours before the scheduled session. If you're unhappy with your itinerary, contact us and we'll work to make it right.

Can I modify my itinerary after purchase? ▾

Your itinerary is a recommendation guide — you can follow it exactly or use it as a starting point. You're free to swap days, skip activities, or add your own. The Booking Concierge service can help adjust plans too.

Do you earn commissions from booking links? ▾

Yes, we may earn a small commission from bookings made through our affiliate links (GetYourGuide, Booking.com, etc.) at no extra cost to you. This helps us keep improving and adding more venues and features.
