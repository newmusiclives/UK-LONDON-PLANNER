Tower of London
Explore nearly 1,000 years of royal history, see the Crown Jewels, and meet the Beefeaters. Arrive early to beat the crowds.
12:30 - 13:30 / Lunch
Padella
Outstanding handmade pasta at unbeatable prices. The pappardelle with 8-hour beef shin ragu is extraordinary.
14:00 - 16:30 / Afternoon
Westminster Abbey & Houses of Parliament
Gothic masterpiece and coronation church since 1066. Walk along the Thames to see Big Ben and Parliament up close.
18:30 - 20:00 / Dinner
Rules Restaurant
London's oldest restaurant (est. 1798). Traditional British fare in opulent Edwardian surroundings. The steak and kidney pie is legendary.
20:30 - 22:30 / Evening
Gordon's Wine Bar
London's oldest wine bar, hidden in a candlelit cave. Atmospheric, romantic, and unmissable. Arrive early for a spot.
