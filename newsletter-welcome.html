3 Free London Insider Tips

These are the tips that most tourists never discover — the kind of local knowledge that saves you money, skips the queues, and makes your trip 10x better.

1
The £1.75 Sightseeing Tour
Save £30+ on sightseeing buses

Forget the £35 open-top tour buses. London's regular red buses pass every major landmark — and they cost just £1.75 per journey with Oyster or contactless.

The Secret Routes

  • Bus 11: Liverpool Street → St Paul's Cathedral → Fleet Street → Strand → Trafalgar Square → Westminster → Victoria. This single route passes more landmarks than most paid tours.
  • Bus 15: Tower of London → Monument → Bank → St Paul's → Aldwych → Trafalgar Square → Piccadilly Circus → Regent Street → Oxford Circus → Paddington.
  • Bus 24: Hampstead Heath → Camden → Mornington Crescent → Warren Street → Tottenham Court Road → Leicester Square → Trafalgar Square → Westminster → Pimlico.

Pro tip: Sit on the top deck at the front for the best views. The Hopper fare means you can hop on and off unlimited buses within 1 hour for just one £1.75 fare. So you can ride Bus 11, jump off at St Paul's for photos, then catch the next Bus 11 — all on one fare.

Savings: £30+ per person vs tour bus
2
The West End Theatre Hack
See world-class shows for up to 70% off

London's West End has the best theatre in the world — but full-price tickets can cost £80-150. Here's how locals get the same seats for a fraction of the price:

The 4-Step System

  • TKTS Booth, Leicester Square — the official discount ticket booth. Same-day tickets at 20-50% off. Opens 10am. Go early for best selection. This is not a scam — it's run by the Society of London Theatre.
  • TodayTix app — last-minute tickets, lottery tickets, and rush tickets. Some shows release £25 front-row seats via daily lottery. Download before you arrive.
  • Day seats — many shows (including Hamilton, Les Mis, Phantom) release a limited number of £20-25 tickets at the box office each morning. Queue from 8am.
  • Matinee performances — Wednesday and Saturday matinees are typically 20-30% cheaper than evening shows, with the same cast.

Insider secret: Tuesday and Wednesday evenings are the cheapest nights for West End shows. Friday and Saturday are the most expensive. Same show, same cast, different price.

Savings: £40-80 per ticket
3
The Free Museum Secret Nobody Tells You
World-class art and history without spending a penny

London has more free museums than any city on Earth. Most tourists know about the British Museum and the Tate — but there are 20+ world-class museums that are completely free:

The Free A-List

  • British Museum — Rosetta Stone, Egyptian mummies, Parthenon marbles. One of the greatest museums ever built. Free.
  • National Gallery — Van Gogh, Monet, Da Vinci, Rembrandt. Trafalgar Square. Free.
  • Tate Modern — Picasso, Warhol, Rothko in a converted power station on the Thames. Free.
  • V&A Museum — 5,000 years of art and design. The building alone is worth the visit. Free.
  • Natural History Museum — dinosaurs, the blue whale, and a Romanesque cathedral of a building. Free.
  • Science Museum — interactive exhibits, IMAX cinema, space exploration. Perfect for kids and adults. Free.
  • Imperial War Museum — powerful WWI and WWII exhibits, Holocaust gallery. Free.
  • Museum of London — the entire history of London from Roman times to today. Free.
  • Wellcome Collection — the intersection of science, medicine, life, and art. Weird, wonderful, free.
  • Wallace Collection — old masters, armour, and French furniture in a Mayfair mansion. Hidden gem. Free.

The timing trick: Most museums are quietest on weekday mornings (10am-12pm) and Friday evenings (many have late openings until 8:30pm with fewer crowds). Avoid weekends and school holidays if you can.

The donation trick: Museums are free but suggest a £5 donation. If there's a long queue for the "suggested donation" entrance, walk straight to the "free entry" door — there usually is one, it's just less obvious.

Savings: £100+ over a week (vs cities where museums charge £15-25 each)

BONUS: The Local's Secret Dinner Hack

Many of London's best restaurants offer set lunch menus at 40-60% less than dinner prices — same chef, same kitchen, same quality. Restaurants like The Ivy, Hawksmoor, and Barrafina all have incredible lunch deals. Eat your "big meal" at lunch and have a lighter dinner at a pub. You'll eat better food for less money, and you'll get into restaurants that are fully booked for dinner.

Download the app "Too Good To Go" — restaurants, bakeries, and cafes sell surplus food at closing time for £3-4. We've picked up £15 meals from top restaurants for under £4.
