A Day in Musical London

Here's one sample day for a rock-and-indie traveller. Yours will be tuned to your taste, pace, and the neighbourhoods you want to sleep in.

  • Morning Abbey Road crossing + St John's Wood breakfast Beat the coach tours by 08:30. Breakfast at a local café before the zebra gets crowded.
  • Midday British Museum The Beatles' original record contract is here — see it, then the Rosetta Stone in the same visit. Two hours is enough.
  • Afternoon Camden record-shop crawl + The Dublin Castle Out of the Present, Sounds That Swing, Flashback Records — then a pint at the pub that launched Madness.
  • Evening Dinner + a gig at KOKO or the Roundhouse Both walkable from Camden. We book the table around your set time — never the other way round.
  • Late Ronnie Scott's late set (Soho) If you're still game. Last tube home or a black cab — the plan has both options costed.

Every day is built like this — researched, timed, and walkable — and yours will look nothing like this one.

Honest Answers

Is this a pre-baked music tour?

No. It's still a fully personalised itinerary — same wizard, same day-by-day planning as our standard trip. The difference is the weighting: the music stops that matter to you become the anchor points, and everything else plans around them.

What if I'm travelling with someone who isn't a music obsessive?

Music is the spine, not the whole meal. You'll still get the Tower, the Tate, Borough Market, the Thames walks — the icons are built in, just routed so they complement the gigs. If your partner wants more sightseeing, you tell us in the wizard and we shift the balance.

Which genres do you cover?

All of them. Classical, opera, jazz, blues, folk, rock, punk, indie, Britpop, electronic, reggae, dub, soul, hip-hop, grime, UK rap, hardcore, metal, hyperpop. If London has venues and history for it — and it does — we can build a trip around it.

Do you book the gigs and venues for me?

We recommend and time the itinerary around what's on during your dates, and link you straight to the booking pages. We don't take commission on tickets — we point you at the same seats you'd buy yourself.

What if nothing I want is on during my dates?

There's always something. London has roughly 900 live music shows a week. If your specific favourite act isn't playing, we suggest comparable acts in the same scene — or you pick dates that do align with what you want.
