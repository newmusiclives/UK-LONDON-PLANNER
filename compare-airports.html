Transport Options by Airport

Heathrow (LHR)

Elizabeth Line (Crossrail): The best-value option at £5.50 with Oyster/contactless. Takes about 45 minutes to central London with air-conditioned, spacious carriages and step-free access.

Heathrow Express: 15-minute non-stop service to Paddington. Costs £25 standard (£5.50 for under-16s). Worth it if you are in a rush or your hotel is near Paddington.

Piccadilly Line: The cheapest at standard Tube fare, but takes 50–60 minutes and is often crowded with luggage.

Bus: National Express coaches from £6, taking 60–90 minutes to Victoria Coach Station.

Gatwick (LGW)

Gatwick Express: 30-minute non-stop to Victoria, £19.90. Clean, frequent (every 15 min), and reliable.

Thameslink: Cheaper at around £12, stops at Blackfriars, City Thameslink, Farringdon, and St Pancras. Takes 35–45 minutes.

Southern Railway: Similar to Thameslink, calling at London Bridge and Victoria. Around £12–16.

Bus: National Express from £5, but can take 90+ minutes in traffic.

Stansted (STN)

Stansted Express: 47 minutes to Liverpool Street, £19.40. Book online in advance for savings of up to 40%.

Bus: National Express and Terravision coaches from £6–10 to Liverpool Street or Victoria. Takes 60–90 minutes depending on traffic.

Luton (LTN)

Thameslink train + DART shuttle: The DART automated shuttle links the airport terminal to Luton Airport Parkway station in 4 minutes (£5.40). From there, Thameslink trains reach St Pancras in about 25 minutes (£10–16).

Bus: National Express from £5 to Victoria. Takes 60–90 minutes. The cheapest option but least predictable timing.

London City (LCY)

DLR (Docklands Light Railway): By far the easiest airport transfer in London. Walk straight from the terminal to the DLR platform. Trains every 4–8 minutes to Bank station (22 min) at standard Oyster/contactless fares. Perfect for business travellers heading to the City or Canary Wharf.

Best Airport for Your Needs

Flying from North America or Asia: Heathrow. The most international connections, the best public transport links, and purpose-built for long-haul passengers.
Budget flights from Europe: Stansted or Luton for the cheapest fares (Ryanair, Wizz Air). Just factor in the transfer cost and time.
Families with young children: Heathrow or Gatwick. Both have superior facilities, family lanes at security, and more reliable transport links.
Business travellers: London City. Smallest airport, fastest check-in, and central location means less wasted time. Heathrow T5 is the runner-up.
Connecting to the Eurostar: Gatwick or Luton — both have Thameslink services that stop at St Pancras International, where Eurostar departs.
Best overall value: Heathrow via the Elizabeth Line. At £5.50 for a comfortable 45-minute ride, it is hard to beat. No booking needed — just tap your contactless card.

Money-Saving Tips

Book train tickets in advance. Stansted Express and Gatwick Express offer significant discounts (up to 40%) when you book online ahead of your travel date.

Consider coaches for budget trips. National Express and Terravision are significantly cheaper than trains from Stansted and Luton. Just allow extra time for traffic.

Avoid taxis unless sharing. A taxi from Heathrow to central London costs £50–90. From Gatwick or Stansted, expect £80–120+. Only worthwhile if splitting between 3–4 people or arriving very late at night.

Use the Elizabeth Line from Heathrow. Since its 2022 opening, there is little reason to take the expensive Heathrow Express unless you urgently need to reach Paddington in 15 minutes.

Late-Night & Early-Morning Arrivals

If your flight arrives late at night or very early in the morning, your transport options shrink considerably. Here is what to know:

Heathrow: The Piccadilly Line runs until approximately 12:30 AM (later on Friday/Saturday Night Tube). The Elizabeth Line stops around 11:30 PM. Night buses (N9) run to central London through the night, taking about 75 minutes. Taxis and Uber are always available.

Gatwick: Thameslink and Southern trains run through the night between Gatwick and London, though with reduced frequency (hourly). This makes Gatwick one of the better airports for late arrivals.

Stansted: The Stansted Express does not run overnight, but National Express coaches operate 24 hours to Liverpool Street and Victoria, departing roughly every 30–60 minutes through the night.

Luton: Thameslink trains run through the night between Luton Airport Parkway and St Pancras, roughly hourly. The DART shuttle from the terminal to the station also runs 24/7.

City: London City Airport closes at 10 PM on weekdays and 1 PM on Sundays, so late arrivals are not an issue here.

Pre-Booking Checklist

Before booking your flights, run through this quick checklist to make sure you are choosing the right airport:

  • Check the total cost: flight + airport transfer. A £30 cheaper flight from Stansted may cost £20 more in transfers than Heathrow.
  • Factor in travel time, especially if you have limited days in London. An hour saved each way is two extra hours of sightseeing.
  • Consider your accommodation location. If staying in East London, City Airport or Stansted may be closer than Heathrow.
  • Check terminal information for your airline — Heathrow's terminals are far apart and you cannot easily walk between them.
  • If arriving late at night, choose an airport with overnight transport (Gatwick, Luton) or budget for a taxi.
  • Book airport transfers in advance where possible — Stansted Express and Gatwick Express are up to 40% cheaper online.
