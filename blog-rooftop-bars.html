London's 15 Best Rooftop Bars & Sky-High Drinks

Sip cocktails above the skyline. From free panoramic gardens to glamorous cocktail terraces, these are the rooftop bars worth booking.

How to Use This Guide

London's rooftop bar scene has exploded over the past decade, and there is now a sky-high spot for every budget and mood. We have grouped these 15 bars into four categories: free entry venues perfect for budget travellers, cocktail-focused bars for a special night out, food-focused rooftops where the menu is as impressive as the view, and budget-friendly options that prove you do not need to spend a fortune to drink above the city.

Key tip: Most high-end rooftop bars require reservations, especially on Friday and Saturday evenings. Book at least a week ahead in summer. Many venues enforce smart-casual dress codes — leave the trainers at the hotel.

Free Entry

1. Sky Garden

Location: 20 Fenchurch Street, EC3M 3BY (Monument / Bank) • Floor: 35th–37th

The view: A full 360-degree panorama across the Thames, Tower Bridge, The Shard, and the sweep of South London stretching to the Surrey Hills. The three-storey indoor garden is lush with ferns, African lilies, and mature trees.

Price range: Free entry (must pre-book). Cocktails from £14, wine from £9. The Sky Pod bar has the best value drinks; Darwin Brasserie and Fenchurch Restaurant are pricier.

Best time to visit: Sunset slots are the most coveted — book exactly when the calendar opens (typically three weeks ahead). Monday mornings are the easiest slots to grab at short notice.

Booking tips: Free tickets release on the Sky Garden website. Set a reminder because popular dates sell out within hours. Walk-ins are occasionally available after 6 pm on weekdays but never rely on this in summer.

2. Netil360

Location: 1 Westgate Street, Hackney, E8 3RL (London Fields rail) • Floor: Rooftop level

The view: An unpolished, creative panorama across East London rooftops to the City skyline. The stripped-back Astroturf terrace is lined with deckchairs and parasols — think hipster beach club above a warehouse.

Price range: Free entry. Craft beers from £5.50, cocktails from £9, wine from £6. Food from rotating street food vendors.

Best time to visit: Sunny weekend afternoons. This is a fair-weather venue that closes (or loses its appeal) in heavy rain. Get there by 2 pm on a sunny Saturday to guarantee a seat.

Booking tips: No reservations — first come, first served. One-in-one-out when full. Bring cash as backup in case card machines struggle.

Cocktail Focused

3. Sushisamba

Location: Heron Tower, 110 Bishopsgate, EC2N 4AY (Liverpool Street) • Floor: 38th–39th

The view: Vertigo-inducing floor-to-ceiling windows and an outdoor terrace with the highest al fresco dining in London. On clear days you can see the Wembley arch to the west and the QE2 bridge to the east.

Price range: Cocktails £16–£22, sake flights £28, small plates £12–£30. Expect to spend £50–£80 per person for drinks and light bites.

Best time to visit: The exterior terrace at golden hour (around 7–8 pm in summer) is spectacular. Weekday lunchtimes are significantly quieter and cheaper with set menus available.

Booking tips: Reserve via their website. Terrace tables are highly sought after — book 2–3 weeks ahead for weekends. Smart-casual dress code strictly enforced.

4. The Aviary

Location: Montcalm Royal London House, 22–25 Finsbury Square, EC2A 1DX (Moorgate) • Floor: 10th

The view: A north-facing cityscape across Finsbury Square to Islington and beyond. The rooftop is elegantly landscaped with olive trees and sheltered seating areas.

Price range: Cocktails £14–£18, wines from £8. Reasonable by rooftop standards.

Best time to visit: After-work drinks on Thursday or Friday, arriving by 5.30 pm to secure an outdoor seat.

Booking tips: Walk-ins are possible midweek but book ahead for weekends. They have heated covered sections for cooler evenings.

5. Savage Garden

Location: DoubleTree by Hilton, 7 Pepys Street, EC3N 4AF (Tower Hill) • Floor: 12th

The view: One of the best Tower of London and Tower Bridge views from any bar in the city. The jungle-themed decor is theatrical without being tacky.

Price range: Cocktails £14–£18, beers from £7. No minimum spend.

Best time to visit: Sunset on a clear evening for the perfect Tower Bridge golden-hour shot. Sunday afternoons are more relaxed with DJ sets.

Booking tips: Book online at least a few days ahead. They release tables in time slots. Window seats go fast — request when booking.

6. Radio Rooftop

Location: ME London Hotel, 336–337 The Strand, WC2R 1HA (Temple / Covent Garden) • Floor: 10th

The view: A sweeping panorama from the London Eye and South Bank across to St Paul's Cathedral. The sleek white terrace feels like a Mediterranean beach club dropped into central London.

Price range: Cocktails £16–£22, bottle service available. This is one of the pricier options on this list.

Best time to visit: Summer evenings around 7 pm when the Thames glows. In winter they have heated igloos (book well ahead).

Booking tips: Reservations essential, especially weekends. They sometimes have minimum spend on peak nights. Check dress code — no sportswear or flip-flops.

7. Madison

Location: One New Change, EC4M 9AF (St Paul's) • Floor: Rooftop

The view: Arguably the most dramatic close-up view of St Paul's Cathedral dome from any bar. The huge open-air terrace also has sightlines across to the Tate Modern and Millennium Bridge.

Price range: Cocktails £14–£19, wines from £8.50, sharing platters £18–£28.

Best time to visit: Friday early evening for after-work crowds with energy, or Saturday afternoon for a more relaxed vibe. The terrace catches sun until late in summer.

Booking tips: Book via their website. Terrace is first come first served unless you book a VIP area. Arrive early on sunny days.

Food Focused

8. Seabird

Location: The Hoxton, Southwark, 32 Blackfriars Road, SE1 8PB (Southwark) • Floor: 14th

The view: Panoramic south-and-north views — you can see both the Shard and St Paul's. The terrace has a raw bar serving some of the freshest seafood in London.

Price range: Oysters £3 each, seafood platters £45–£85, cocktails £13–£16. Great value for the quality.

Best time to visit: Weekday lunch for the £25 set menu — one of London's best-value rooftop dining deals. Sunset on warm evenings is magical.

Booking tips: Book via The Hoxton website. The outdoor terrace fills fast on sunny days, but the floor-to-ceiling indoor windows are just as good for views.

9. Boundary Rooftop

Location: 2–4 Boundary Street, Shoreditch, E2 7DD (Shoreditch High Street) • Floor: Rooftop

The view: A charming, greenhouse-style rooftop garden overlooking Shoreditch and the City skyline. Less about dizzying heights, more about a beautifully designed space with climbing plants and fairy lights.

Price range: Wood-fired pizzas £12–£16, grilled dishes £14–£22, cocktails £13–£15. Very fairly priced for the setting.

Best time to visit: Long summer lunches are the sweet spot. The heated marquee extends the season into autumn. Sunday roasts on the roof are a local favourite.

Booking tips: Book via their website or OpenTable. The space is intimate (about 50 covers), so reservations are essential at weekends.

10. The Rooftop St James

Location: The Trafalgar St James Hotel, 2 Spring Gardens, SW1A 2TS (Charing Cross) • Floor: Rooftop

The view: Directly overlooking Trafalgar Square and Nelson's Column — one of the most iconic vantage points in London. You can see Big Ben, the London Eye, and down Whitehall from the terrace.

Price range: Cocktails £16–£20, small plates £10–£18. The sharing boards are decent value for central London.

Best time to visit: Late afternoon into evening, when the fountains below are lit and the National Gallery glows. New Year's Eve is legendary but requires an expensive ticket.

Booking tips: Book directly with the hotel. They sometimes allow walk-ins midweek but never on weekends or holidays.

11. Wagtail

Location: 6th Floor, 70 Wilson Street, EC2A 2DB (Moorgate / Old Street) • Floor: 6th

The view: A chic Mediterranean-inspired rooftop with retractable glass roof, overlooking the Barbican and the tech-hub bustle of Old Street. More sheltered than many rooftops, making it year-round viable.

Price range: Mediterranean small plates £8–£16, grilled mains £18–£28, cocktails £13–£16.

Best time to visit: Thursday and Friday evenings for atmosphere. Weekday lunches for a quieter dining experience with the same views.

Booking tips: Book via their website. The retractable roof means weather rarely disrupts plans, making this a solid year-round choice.

Budget-Friendly

12. Frank's Cafe, Peckham

Location: Bold Tendencies Car Park, 95A Rye Lane, Peckham, SE15 4ST (Peckham Rye rail) • Floor: 10th (car park roof)

The view: A sprawling panorama across London from an unlikely multi-storey car park. The Shard, the Gherkin, and Canary Wharf all line the horizon. The industrial setting — complete with concrete pillars and rooftop sculptures from the Bold Tendencies art programme — is iconic South London.

Price range: Beers from £4.50, wine from £5, Aperol spritz £8. The most affordable rooftop drinking in London, hands down.

Best time to visit: Summer only (typically May–September). Arrive before 5 pm on a Saturday to avoid the queue. Sunset here is a rite of passage for Londoners.

Booking tips: No bookings — queue on arrival. The queue moves steadily. Bring layers because it gets windy up there after dark.

13. Skylight

Location: Tobacco Dock, Pennington Street, Wapping, E1W 2SF (Shadwell / Wapping) • Floor: Rooftop

The view: A sprawling rooftop complex with croquet lawns, pétanque courts, and a rooftop pool in summer. Views across Wapping to Canary Wharf and the river.

Price range: Cocktails from £11, beers from £5.50, street food from £8. Activity costs extra (croquet £10 per group, pool sessions ticketed).

Best time to visit: Saturday afternoons in summer for the full experience. They run seasonal events — rooftop cinema in summer, ice skating in winter.

Booking tips: Free entry but activity sessions should be booked online. Arrive early on hot days as capacity limits apply.

14. Golden Bee

Location: 15th Floor, The Mandrake Hotel area, Shoreditch, EC2A (Shoreditch High Street) • Floor: 15th

The view: East London rooftops stretching to the Olympic Park, with the City cluster of skyscrapers to the west. Stylish gold-and-botanical decor with a buzzy atmosphere.

Price range: Cocktails £12–£16, beers from £6, bar snacks from £6. Mid-range pricing with regular happy hour deals.

Best time to visit: Happy hour (varying times, check their socials) for best value. Weekday evenings are lively without being rammed.

Booking tips: Walk-ins welcome but booking recommended for groups. They have DJs on weekends which can mean minimum spend or entry fee after 10 pm.

15. Bussey Rooftop Bar

Location: CLF Art Cafe, 133 Rye Lane, Peckham, SE15 4BQ (Peckham Rye rail) • Floor: Rooftop

The view: Similar panorama to Frank's Cafe (they are neighbours) but with a slightly different angle and a more party-focused vibe. Regular DJ sets and live music events on the roof.

Price range: Beers from £4, cocktails from £8, street food from £6. Entry sometimes charged for weekend evening events (£5–£10).

Best time to visit: Weekend afternoons for chilled drinking, weekend nights for rooftop parties. Check CLF Art Cafe listings for special events.

Booking tips: No reservations for the bar itself. Event tickets available on their website or Dice. Bring ID — door policy can be strict for evening events.

Practical Tips for London Rooftop Bars

  • Weather: London rooftop season runs roughly May to September, though many venues have heated or covered areas for year-round use. Always check if your chosen bar has shelter before visiting in cooler months.
  • Dress code: Most upscale rooftop bars enforce smart-casual. No sportswear, no flip-flops, no ripped jeans. Frank's Cafe and Netil360 are the exceptions — wear whatever you like.
  • Booking: Reserve at least a week ahead for weekend evenings in summer. Many venues now use time-slot bookings (typically 2-hour slots).
  • Budget: Expect to spend £15–£20 per cocktail at high-end venues. Frank's Cafe and Bussey Rooftop are the best options for drinks under £10.
  • Photography: Golden hour (roughly 7–8.30 pm in midsummer) gives the best light. Sky Garden and Madison are the most photogenic for skyline shots.
  • Accessibility: All the hotel-based rooftops have lift access. The car park rooftops (Frank's, Bussey) have stairs and may have limited accessibility — call ahead to check.

Suggested Rooftop Bar Crawl

If you want to hit multiple spots in one evening, here is a realistic route through the City and East London:

  1. 5 pm — Sky Garden (free, pre-booked): Start with the panoramic views and a drink at Sky Pod bar.
  2. 6.30 pm — Savage Garden (10-min walk): Tower Bridge views and a cocktail or two.
  3. 8.30 pm — Golden Bee (15 min via Tube to Shoreditch High Street): East London skyline as the sun sets.
  4. 10 pm — Boundary Rooftop (5-min walk): End with late-night pizza and a nightcap in the fairy-lit garden.

Total cost estimate: £50–£80 per person including drinks and a pizza to share.
