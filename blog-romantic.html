Most Romantic Things to Do in London

From candlelit wine bars to Thames sunset walks — London is one of the most romantic cities in the world

Romantic Walks

  • South Bank at sunset — walk from Westminster Bridge to Tower Bridge as the city lights up. Stop at the Southbank Centre book market and watch the Thames sparkle.
  • Hampstead Heath to Parliament Hill — climb to the viewpoint for panoramic London skyline views. Combine with a cosy pub lunch at The Holly Bush.
  • Regent's Canal, Little Venice to Camden — a hidden waterway walk through London's prettiest canal section. Stop at a canalside cafe.
  • Kew Gardens — 300 acres of stunning gardens. The Palm House, Treetop Walkway, and Japanese Garden are breathtaking. Especially magical in spring and autumn.
  • Primrose Hill at sunset — the best free panoramic view of London. Bring a bottle of wine and watch the sun set over the skyline.

Romantic Dining

  • Clos Maggiore, Covent Garden — voted "most romantic restaurant in the world." Fireplace, flower-covered conservatory, French cuisine. Book well ahead.
  • Duck & Waffle — London's highest restaurant, open 24/7. Stunning City views for sunrise or sunset dining.
  • Andrew Edmunds, Soho — candlelit, intimate, with handwritten menus and exceptional wine. A Soho institution since 1985.
  • Rules, Covent Garden — London's oldest restaurant (1798). Opulent Edwardian rooms, traditional British fare, and literary history.
  • Thames dinner cruise — multi-course dinner while floating past illuminated Parliament, the Eye, and Tower Bridge.

Romantic Bars & Evenings

  • Gordon's Wine Bar — London's oldest wine bar, hidden in a candlelit cave. Atmospheric, intimate, and unforgettable. Cash only inside.
  • Nightjar, Shoreditch — one of the world's top cocktail bars. Live jazz, 1920s decor, and theatrical cocktails. Reservations essential.
  • The Connaught Bar, Mayfair — regularly voted the world's best bar. Art Deco elegance and martini trolley service.
  • Oriole, Farringdon — hidden beneath Smithfield Market. Live world music and three themed rooms. Every cocktail tells a story.
  • The Dove, Hammersmith — 18th-century riverside pub with a tiny bar (Guinness World Record smallest) and Thames terrace at sunset.

Romantic Experiences

  • AIRE Ancient Baths — candlelit thermal baths in a 600-year-old building near Covent Garden. Book the wine bath for maximum romance.
  • London Helicopter Tour — 12-15 minute flight over all of London's landmarks. Perfect for proposals.
  • Electric Cinema, Notting Hill — plush leather armchairs, cashmere blankets, and cocktails delivered to your seat. Book the front-row double beds.
  • Afternoon Tea at The Ritz — the quintessential British romantic experience. Book 6 weeks ahead. Smart dress code.
  • Hot Tub Boat on the Thames — a wood-fired hot tub floating past London's landmarks. Surreal and brilliant. BYO champagne.

Best Proposal Spots

  • The Shard viewing gallery at sunset
  • Primrose Hill with the skyline behind you
  • Kew Gardens in the Japanese Garden
  • Tower Bridge walkway with Thames views
  • During a helicopter tour over London
