Canary Wharf Neighbourhood Guide

Docklands skyline, the West India Docks, and the modern London that tourists miss — high finance meets riverside parkland

Overview

Canary Wharf sits on the Isle of Dogs — a Thames peninsula where the West India Docks operated 1802-1980. In the 1990s the old warehouses and docks became the centre of a new business district: 18 million sq ft of offices, 40+ skyscrapers, 400 shops, and a waterside public realm that's actually lovely.

For tourists: the Museum of London Docklands (free, in a Georgian sugar warehouse), the cable car across the Thames, the Crossrail Place roof garden, and photography of London's most ambitious modern skyline.

The vibe: Glass-and-steel professional by day; surprisingly lively for drinks after 5pm; quiet weekends.

Best for: Architecture fans, photographers (especially at dusk), families (cable car, Mudchute Farm), business visitors.

Top Attractions

  • 1. Museum of London Docklands

    No 1 Warehouse, West India Quay, E14 4AL

    Free museum of the Thames, the port, and the docks — inside a restored Georgian sugar warehouse. "Sailortown" reconstruction is memorable.

  • 2. IFS Cloud Cable Car

    Edmund Halley Way, SE10 0FR

    A 1.1km cable car across the Thames from Greenwich Peninsula to the Royal Docks — 90 metres at the highest point.

  • 3. Crossrail Place Roof Garden

    Crossrail Place, E14 5AR

    Foster + Partners' timber-latticed rooftop park — divided into eastern and western plant hemispheres, suspended over the Crossrail station.

  • 4. Mudchute Park and Farm

    Pier St, E14 3HP

    A 32-acre urban farm in the middle of the Isle of Dogs — free, family-friendly, with London's skyline as a backdrop.

  • 5. Canary Wharf Public Art Trail

    Across the Canary Wharf estate

    Free self-guided walk of 70+ sculptures by Lynn Chadwick, Henry Moore, Pierre Vivant, and others. Maps at Reception Centre.

Best Restaurants

  • 1. Dishoom Canary Wharf

    22 Hertsmere Rd, E14 4ED · Indian · $$

    The airy Canary Wharf Dishoom — less-queued than central branches. Breakfast is the move.

  • 2. Boisdale of Canary Wharf

    Cabot Pl, E14 4QT · Scottish · $$$$

    Scottish restaurant specialising in steaks, oysters, and whisky — live jazz nightly in the sprawling upstairs lounge.

  • 3. Roka Canary Wharf

    Level 1, 40 Canada Square, E14 5FW · Japanese · $$$$

    The Canary Wharf Roka — robatayaki grill, sushi, glamorous atmosphere. Set lunch £35.

  • 4. Iberica Canary Wharf

    Cabot Square, E14 4QT · Spanish · $$$

    Airy Spanish restaurant in the Financial Times building — jamón counter, great tapas, weekend brunch.

  • 5. Plateau

    4th Floor, Canada Place, E14 5ER · French · $$$$

    Glass-walled fourth-floor French restaurant with panoramic skyline views — a Canary Wharf fixture for business lunches.

Best Bars & Pubs

  • 1. The Gun

    27 Coldharbour, E14 9NS

    A smart Georgian riverside pub in the Isle of Dogs — Lord Nelson and Lady Hamilton used to meet here. Summer terrace with O2 views.

  • 2. The Jamaica Wine House

    Wine Office Ct, City (short DLR ride)

    Historic City pub — worth the short trip for a drink before heading back to Canary Wharf.

  • 3. Sipping House Canary Wharf

    Park Pavilion, 40 Canada Square, E14 5FW

    Garden pavilion bar with outdoor seating — excellent cocktails, lovely summer terrace.

  • 4. Humble Grape Canary Wharf

    1 Westferry Circus, E14 4HD

    Wine bar-restaurant with 400+ wines and a serious but approachable list. Retail+£15 corkage.

  • 5. The Pepper Saint Ontiod

    27 Marsh Wall, E14 9SH

    Quirky-themed cocktail bar in the middle of the Canary Wharf estate — loud, lively, popular with the suit crowd.

Best Cafes

  • 1. Watch House Canary Wharf

    Crossrail Place, E14 5AR

    Specialty coffee and cold brew — reliable morning stop.

  • 2. The Breakfast Club Canary Wharf

    34 Westferry Circus, E14 8RR

    All-day American-British brunch cafe — pancake stacks, huevos rancheros, Canary Wharf weekend favourite.

  • 3. Kongs

    Cabot Square, E14 4QQ

    Hidden under Cabot Square — Indonesian-Malaysian flavours for breakfast and lunch, a Canary Wharf secret.

Shopping Highlights

  • Canary Wharf Shopping — Cabot Place, Canada Place, and Jubilee Place malls cover 320+ shops with luxury and high-street fashion.
  • Waitrose Canary Wharf — the flagship of Waitrose's premium grocery.
  • The Ritzy Gift Bar (Cabot Place) — bespoke fragrance and candle boutique.
  • The Everyman Cinema Canary Wharf — boutique cinema with sofa seating, meals, cocktails.
  • Canary Wharf Shopping App — click and collect across 300+ stores delivered to the Reception Centre.

Getting There

Main stations serving this neighbourhood:

  • Canary Wharf (Jubilee, Elizabeth) — the hub station
  • Heron Quays / South Quay / Crossharbour (DLR) — Isle of Dogs south side
  • Canada Water (Jubilee, Overground) — two stops west — Greenwich connection
  • North Greenwich (Jubilee) — one stop east — O2 Arena

Insider Tips

  • Crossrail Place Roof Garden is free — one of London's nicest free public spaces.
  • The cable car is £6 one-way and best at dusk — Thames-level views of the City and Canary Wharf skylines.
  • Weekends are quiet — restaurants that require bookings weekdays can be walked into.
  • Canary Wharf to Greenwich via the Thames Clippers boat is one of the loveliest 15-minute commutes in London.
  • Boisdale has live jazz nightly (free) — worth a drink even without dinner.
